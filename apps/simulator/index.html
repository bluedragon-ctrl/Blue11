Time

        
        
How fast the machine runs against real time. The machine counts time in frames of 20000 cycles, one tick of its 60 Hz line clock each: at real time 60 run per second of your time. A program behaves exactly the same at every speed; its sound plays slower and lower, or faster and higher, like a tape.

        
Sound

        
        
The speaker (SNDCS, SNDDIV). At real time it plays at its true pitch; slower is lower, faster is higher.

        
        
The machine (changing one restarts the program)

        
        
The real beam draws the picture row by row while the program runs, so changing the page or palette mid-frame tears.

        
        
The Extended Instruction Set: MUL, DIV, ASH, ASHC.

        
Look

        
        
The look of the period: the screen as a CRT, the listings printed on fan-fold paper, the console as an amber terminal. The editor and the debugger stay as they are.
